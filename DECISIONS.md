# Decisions

This is the part we read most carefully. Keep it short — notes, not an essay.
Bullet points are fine. Half a page per ticket is plenty.

---

## What I assumed

Where a ticket did not say, what did you decide and why? What would you have
asked the reporter if they had been available?

## What I changed

A sentence or two per ticket. The commits cover the detail.

## What I deliberately did not do

Things you noticed and left alone, and the reasoning. This carries as much
weight here as the code does.

## Trade-offs

Anywhere you picked one approach over another and it was a close call. What
would change your mind?

## What I would do next

With another day, in priority order.

## Where I used AI

Which tools, what you handed to them, and where you overruled them or threw the
output away. Asked in good faith — we use these tools too and we are interested
in how you drive them.

## Anything broken or unfinished

Say so plainly. This costs you nothing here.
